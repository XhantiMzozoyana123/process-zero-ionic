## 1. Getting Started

### Registration & Login

1. Open the Process Zero app and tap **Register** to create your account.
2. Enter your name, email address, and a strong password.
3. Check your email for a confirmation link and verify your address.
4. Log in. If **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** is enabled on your account, you will be prompted to enter a verification code.

### Your First Steps (Milestone Guide)

When you log in for the first time, a **Milestone Guide** appears on your dashboard. It walks you through five key actions to get you up and running:

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Complete a Product Assessment |
| 2 | Browse the Lead Lake |
| 3 | Add your first Contact |
| 4 | Book a Meeting |
| 5 | Update your KPI |

Complete each milestone to unlock the full platform. You can track your progress with the visual progress bar at the top of your dashboard.

> **Note:** Most features are locked until you pass at least one Product Assessment. Start there.

## 2. Dashboard

**Path:** Home → Dashboard

The dashboard is your daily command centre. At a glance you can see:

- **KPI Intelligence Report** — your latest performance metrics across all the product lines you are qualified to sell.
- **Latest KPI Snapshot** — a summary card showing your most recent daily activity numbers.
- **Milestone Progress** — your onboarding checklist and how far along you are.
- **Quick Links** — fast navigation to Products, Lead Lake, and Meetings.

### Reading Your KPI Cards

Each product line you are qualified for displays a KPI card showing:

- Call and email outreach targets vs actuals
- Meetings booked
- Deals closed (rand value)
- Active clients
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)

Tap any card to drill into the full detail view for that product.

## 3. Product Assessments

**Path:** Sidebar → Assessments

Before you can sell a product, you must **pass its assessment**. This proves you understand what you are selling.

### How to Take an Assessment

1. Go to **Assessments** in the sidebar.
2. You will see a list of available assessments — both general platform assessments and product-specific ones.
3. Tap an assessment to begin.
4. Read the welcome screen, then tap **Start**.
5. Answer each question. Questions are either:
   - **Multiple Choice** — select the best answer from the options provided.
   - **Open-Ended** — type your answer in the text field.
6. Use the stepper at the top to track your progress (e.g., Question 3 of 10).
7. Tap **Submit** when you have answered all questions.
8. Your score and pass/fail result will be shown immediately.

### What Happens After You Pass

- The product is unlocked in your **Products** page.
- You can now book meetings and track KPIs for that product.
- Your qualification status is visible to your manager.

> If you fail, you can retake the assessment. Review the training videos for the product first.

## 4. Lead Lake

**Path:** Sidebar → Lead Lake

The Lead Lake is a database of pre-loaded prospects you can turn into contacts.

### Searching for Leads

Use the filters at the top of the page to narrow down leads:

| Filter | What it does |
|--------|-------------|
| **Search** | Filter by name, email, company, or location |
| **Industry** | Technology, Finance, Healthcare, Education, Retail, Manufacturing, Entertainment, Hospitality, Transportation, Energy, Other |
| **Intent Level** | High, Medium, Low — a signal of how likely the prospect is to buy |

### Adding Leads as Contacts

1. Find a lead that looks promising.
2. Tap the lead to view their details (name, email, phone, company, job title, location).
3. Tap **Add as Contact** to create a contact record for that person.
4. You can also select multiple leads and tap **Add Selected** to batch-import them.

> Leads with **High intent** should be your first priority — they are the most likely to convert.

## 5. Contacts

**Path:** Sidebar → My Contacts

Contacts are your prospects and clients. Every deal starts with a contact.

### Adding a Contact Manually

1. Tap the **+ New Contact** button.
2. Fill in the details:
   - First Name, Last Name (required)
   - Email, Phone
   - Company, Job Title, Location
   - Industry
3. Tap **Save**.

### Contact Statuses

Keep your contacts up to date by setting the right status:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Reached** | You have made initial contact |
| **Follow Up** | Needs a follow-up call or email |
| **Converted** | Deal closed — they are now a client |
| **Active** | Ongoing active client relationship |
| **Deactivated** | No longer pursuing this contact |

### Tracking Revenue

On each contact record you can log a **Closed Amount** (in Rands). This feeds into your KPI metrics automatically — your MRR and deal-size figures update when you save.

### Searching & Viewing Contacts

- Use the **search bar** to filter contacts by any field.
- Switch between **Table view** (compact rows) and **Card view** (visual tiles) using the toggle buttons.
- Contacts are paginated — use the navigation at the bottom to browse large lists.

## 6. Meetings

**Path:** Sidebar → Meetings

Use the Meetings section to schedule, track, and manage all your sales meetings.

### Booking a Meeting

1. Tap **+ New Meeting**.
2. Select a **Contact** from your contacts list.
3. Select the **Product** you want to discuss.
4. Choose your **Timezone** (19+ timezones supported).
5. Pick a **Date** — weekends are automatically blocked.
6. Available **time slots** will load from Cal.com. Select a slot.
7. Add optional **Notes**.
8. Tap **Book Meeting**.

The meeting is saved to your list and a Cal.com booking is created automatically.

### Managing Meetings

- **View:** All your meetings appear in a searchable list with the contact name, product, date, and time.
- **Edit:** Tap any meeting to update the notes or details.
- **Cancel:** Delete a meeting if it is no longer happening.
- Use **Table** or **Card** view and the pagination controls to navigate large lists.

> Booking a meeting automatically counts towards your **Meetings Booked** KPI for the day.

## 8. Relay AI — Email Automation

**Path:** Sidebar → Relay AI

Relay AI is your automated email outreach engine. It connects to your Gmail account and sends cold emails on your behalf — every day, automatically.

### Getting Connected

1. Go to **Relay AI** in the sidebar.
2. Tap the **Accounts** tab.
3. Tap **Connect Google Account**.
4. Sign in with your Gmail account and grant the required permissions.
5. Your account will appear in the list with a **health score**.

### What Relay AI Does For You

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Automated Sending** | AI writes and sends cold outreach emails daily |
| **Smart Volume Control** | Manages sending rate to protect your inbox reputation |
| **Reply Sorting** | Automatically categorises incoming replies |
| **Continuous Learning** | Improves email copy based on what gets responses |
| **Activity Monitoring** | Tracks emails sent, reply rate, and inbox health per account |

### Viewing Your Stats

Switch to the **Overview** tab to see aggregated sending statistics across all your connected accounts — total emails sent, reply rates, and account health scores.

> A healthy inbox score keeps your emails landing in inboxes, not spam folders. Relay AI manages this automatically.

## 9. KPI Policies

**Path:** Sidebar → KPI Policies

KPI Policies are the performance targets set by your manager. They define what is expected of you for each product.

### What to Look For

| Policy Field | What it means |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Daily emails target** | How many outreach emails you should send per day |
| **Daily calls target** | How many calls you should make per day |
| **Weekly meetings target** | Minimum meetings to book per week |
| **Monthly revenue target** | Total deal value to close per month |
| **Monthly MRR target** | Monthly recurring revenue to generate |
| **Minimum reply rate** | Percentage of outreach emails that should get replies |

### Consequences of Missing Targets

Policies also define what happens if you consistently miss your targets:

| Consequence Level | What it means |
|------------------|---------------|
| **None** | Informational only |
| **Warning** | A warning is issued |
| **Freeze** | Account features may be restricted |
| **Suspend** | Account access is suspended pending manager review |

Use the KPI Policies page to understand your targets and stay ahead of them.

## 12. Tips & Best Practices

### Daily Routine

1. **Start on the Dashboard** — check your KPI snapshot and see where you stand against targets.
2. **Check the Lead Lake** — filter for High-intent leads in your target industry.
3. **Update Contact statuses** — keep your pipeline accurate after any calls or emails.
4. **Book meetings** — try to hit your weekly meeting target by spreading bookings through the week.
5. **Let Relay AI run** — it sends outreach for you in the background. Check your reply stats in the Overview tab.

### Improving Your Reply Rate

- Connect a **dedicated sending account** to Relay AI rather than your personal inbox.
- Allow Relay AI to warm up the account for the first 1–2 weeks before expecting high volume.
- Keep your **contact statuses updated** — it helps Relay AI personalise outreach.

### Staying Qualified

- Assessments may be updated when products change. Check the Assessments page regularly.
- Watch the Training Videos before re-taking any assessment you failed.

### Getting Help

- Tap **Help & Guide** in the Settings menu or sidebar for in-app documentation.
- Contact your manager if your account is frozen or suspended.
